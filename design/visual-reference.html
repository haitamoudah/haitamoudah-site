work / 01

ascend

a personal dashboard for windows. weighted habits with per-day schedules, routines and exercises, and body composition worked out on your own machine. three releases so far.

i built it offline-only on purpose. most apps in this space are designed to bring you back: streaks that guilt you, notifications timed for when you've gone quiet. ascend can't send you a re-engagement email because it has no idea what one is.

ascend v0.5.0windows
enginepure scoring. streak multipliers, partial credit, and effective-dated parameters, so changing a setting never rewrites past scores
storagea single sqlite file on disk, with daily snapshot backups and one-click restore
networknone. no accounts, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no calls out
mathmifflin-st jeor for resting burn, us navy tape method for body fat. computed locally and labelled with their error ranges, because they're trend lines, not verdicts
shippingthe installer isn't code-signed yet, so windows throws a smartscreen prompt. every release publishes its sha256 so you can check what you downloaded is what i built
